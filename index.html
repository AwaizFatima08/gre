Take the GRE before the GRE

Full-length, timed GRE General Test simulations in the current shorter format: the Issue essay, two Verbal and two Quantitative sections, and a second section in each measure that gets harder or easier depending on how you did in the first. Then estimated scores and an explanation for every question.

Get it on Google Play

20 full-length mock tests

The real pattern

Analyze an Issue (30 min), Verbal 12 + 15 questions (18 + 23 min) and Quant 12 + 15 questions (21 + 26 min). Mark and review, a section clock and an on-screen calculator for Quant.

Adaptive like the real test

Section 1 of each measure decides whether you get the easier or harder section 2, and only the harder one opens the top of the 130-170 scale.

Scores and explanations

Estimated Verbal and Quant scores, results by section, question type and topic, and why each answer is right. Rate your essay against six criteria and a model essay.

What you get

  • Every question type. Text completion (one, two and three blanks), sentence equivalence, reading comprehension with select-all and select-in-passage, quantitative comparison, numeric entry and data interpretation with tables and charts.
  • Lessons for every topic. Methods, worked examples and the traps the test sets, from integers to probability and from argument paragraphs to the Issue essay.
  • Topic practice. Separate practice questions for each topic with instant answers, so practice never spoils a mock.
  • Essay practice. Timed Issue prompts with model essays and what a strong response does.
  • Vocabulary flashcards. High-frequency GRE words with meanings, examples and synonyms.
  • Tips, strategy and study plans. Time management, guessing and reviewing, test day, and 4, 8 and 12-week plans.
  • Private and offline. No account. Your scores and essays stay on your phone.
